### Added

- OpenCode 2 lead team tools `team_create`, `team_delete`, `task_create`, `task_list`, `task_get` and `task_update` for root sessions. Each call builds the Native team service from that call's own operation and authority, starts members in-process, and keeps team storage under the lead's state directory. The lead's public `task_send` routes team messages and shutdown requests through Native. Children never receive the lead tools.

### Fixed

- OpenCode 2 `task_output` (and any later operation on the lead's tasks) no longer fails with "Accepted Native custody unavailable" after `task_cancel` releases a finished child. A release the runtime made itself now leaves a receipt bound to the exact journal identity and revision, so the result stays readable as the cancel reply promises, while changed or foreign rows are still refused.
