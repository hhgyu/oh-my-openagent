### Changed

- The Native team service moved from the Senpi adapter into `@oh-my-opencode/senpi-task` behind a new `./team-operations` export. It takes the lead session and the member launch configuration as explicit dependencies, so host adapters other than Senpi can build it. The Senpi adapter wraps it unchanged.

## 2026-11-11 - Team service in senpi-task (#6)

`createTeamService`, `createTeamMailboxReconciler`, `createLeadPoller`, `buildLeadTeamTools` and `TEAM_LEAD_SENTINEL` are exported from `senpi-task/team-operations`; unused root re-exports were removed. Senpi's team service is a thin adapter passing `leadSessionId` and a process `memberLaunch`. A bundle test pins the team service to the lazy task sidecar.
