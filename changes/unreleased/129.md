## 2026-11-27 - Treat a zombie as dead in the process sweep liveness check (#129)

The shared liveness check reads the process state (`/proc` on Linux, an abortable `ps` on other POSIX systems), so a terminated process waiting for its reaper is reported as stopped instead of surviving. A `ps` that fails to observe the process keeps it alive, and the probe follows the sweep's abort and stop bound.
