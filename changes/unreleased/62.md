### Fixed

- OpenCode 2 `task` children are reported only when their session is really idle: a child that ends its turn while its own background job runs is held, the run the job's notice starts continues the turn, and the parent gets one completion notice with that run's answer (background and foreground alike). A failed run settles at once when nothing of the child's own is pending; a stopped held turn settles as cancelled.
- After an OpenCode server restart, the parent's next `task`, `task_send` or `task_cancel` call restores every child that the restart parked or stranded under the dead host, instead of refusing them; a turn the restart cut off, including one held for a background job that died with the host, continues once with Native's restart continuation.

### Changed

- The held turn settlement and the wake source ledger are a harness-neutral `senpi-task` core shared by both Senpi runners and the OpenCode 2 observer; Senpi behavior is unchanged.
