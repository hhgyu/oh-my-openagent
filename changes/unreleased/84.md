### Fixed

- opencode-qa helpers can no longer leave orphaned `opencode serve` processes behind. Servers and fake providers now start through `scripts/lib/serve-guard.mjs` (a Windows kill-on-close Job Object, or a POSIX process group, plus a watch on the driver pid), so they stop with the driver even when the driver is SIGKILLed or `taskkill /F`ed. Each helper's self-test now proves that no server survives a forced driver kill.
