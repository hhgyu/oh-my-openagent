## 2026-11-12 - OpenCode 2 CodeMode fetch GET for webfetch children (#20)

S9b of the OpenCode 2 CodeMode port. `native-task/child-guard-binding-network.ts` binds the owned-child guard's `network` grant only when the approved selection includes Native `webfetch` (or `web_fetch`) resolving to one host tool: `allowsGet` ports senpi `webfetch` `validateUrl` (an `http://` or `https://` prefix and a parseable URL, no host list), and `intrinsicGetOriginal` is the pre-wrap original `webfetch` executor that `owned-child-tool-guard.ts` now exposes to the trusted binder through `intrinsicGetOriginal(hostID)`. The guard's existing `webfetch` hook keeps the user's allow/ask/deny for the URL, refuses non-GET methods and refuses again once the child is revoked or closed. References: https://github.com/hhgyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/20.
