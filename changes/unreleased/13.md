### Changed

- The DAG engine in `@oh-my-opencode/senpi-task/dag` now depends on two narrow ports instead of the whole task manager: `DagTaskPort` (the nine manager methods it calls) and `DagLeasePort` (run lease holder and liveness). Senpi passes its real manager and the pid lease unchanged. No behavior change.
