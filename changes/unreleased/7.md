### Added

- OpenCode 2 team members can run as in-process owned child sessions. A member's Native `task_send` is bound to the dedicated `omo_team_send` host tool through a per-child producer. That producer reloads team-core state before every send and is revoked when the member leaves the team, the team ends or the child closes. Parents and non-member children never see the tool.

### Changed

- The senpi-task team service accepts an `in-process` member launch alongside the existing process launch; Senpi keeps launching members as processes.
