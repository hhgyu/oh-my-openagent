### Added

- OpenCode 2 Native children granted `ls` (for example explore, librarian and the reviewers) can list directories again through OpenCode's CodeMode `execute`. The new `omo_native_ls` tool is a read-only port of Native `ls` with its own `omo_native_ls` permission; it is visible and executable only in a Native child issued with that grant. CodeMode `fetch` from a guarded child is always refused for now.

## 2026-11-12 - OpenCode 2 CodeMode read-only counterpart (#18)

S9a of the OpenCode 2 CodeMode port. `v2/tools/codemode-readonly.ts` registers `omo_native_ls` with `codemode:true`, and `v2/tools/codemode-readonly-ls.ts` ports the senpi `ls` listing (ordering, `/` suffixes, 500-entry and 50KB limits, notices, error texts) without bundling the senpi runtime. `native-task/live-codemode-readonly.ts` owns the registration and one producer per owned child; it is acquired before the owned-child guard so every inner CodeMode call is wrapped by the grant check. The native-task component passes `ls` as a custom counterpart to the host grant, hides the tool from unbound sessions, and binds `codemode-network-guard` as the guard's deny-all `fetch` (no network producer yet). References: https://github.com/hhgyu/oh-my-openagent/pull/18.
