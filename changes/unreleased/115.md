## 2026-11-25 - Apply offline-queued messages after a gated restart (#115)

The receiving drain held its first pass while another process's wake carrier was opening the session. When the carrier gave up, nothing ran the drain again. The held pass now checks the gate every second and runs once the gate lifts. The offline-queue live driver now checks the public contract: `status: "live"` in the thread list, `queued_offline` from a fresh CLI send, and `queued` waiting on a wake from a sender that runs the carrier.
