### Added

- OpenCode 2 team mail delivery: member messages to the lead and lead messages to members are delivered into the recipient session exactly once through a gated `session.synthetic` with a deterministic id, journaled as intent then ACK, re-checking before every delivery that the team is live, the lead is the receiver, the receiver session exists and the member is still bound. Released members get no stray turns, and a deleted team stops its pollers and drops its journal rows.

### Changed

- The senpi-task lead poller and member self-poller accept an optional `isDelivered` port; without it the existing session-file scan is unchanged.

## 2026-11-11 - OpenCode 2 exactly-once team delivery (#9)

`native-team/lead-delivery.ts` and `host/team-delivery-journal.ts` deliver team mail with `delivery:"steer", resume:true` (Native steer/triggerTurn) and ids `msg_omo_team_<sha256 prefix>`, relying on the host's id dedup proven by a probe. Actual isolated OpenCode 2.2.6 runs with a local mock model cover both directions, a forced re-poll, a released member, delete with journal cleanup and the S5c regressions.
